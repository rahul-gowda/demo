Schedule a wellness visit

Having a wellness visit with your primary care provider (PCP) is essential for staying on top of your health. During these important checkups, your provider can:

  • Identify potential health issues.
  • Make sure you are up to date on your screenings and vaccinations.
  • Review any changes in your life or health.
  • Review your mental and behavioral health.

Having these visits may also help you save on heatlh care costs. How? Addressing health issues before they become serious, and so you can avoid higher medical costs later.

There are two easy ways to make an appointment:

  • Call your PCP to schedule a visit during a time that works for you.
  • Use the MyUPMC app. After you log in, select Appointments, then pick a date and time that fit your schedule. This is applicable for UPMC providers only.

Your coverage

Paying for your health care and plan documents

Before you seek care from providers, we recommend that you review your plan documents. You can find them by going to the Coverage Overview on UPMC Health Plan member site or logging in to the UPMC Health Plan app.


After you select your plan, you will see a grid that outlines some of your most commonly used benefits, copayments for different types of providers, your coinsurance, and your deductible (if applicable).

  • Lists health care services and their level of coverage.

PREVENTIVE VERSUS DIAGNOSTIC: WHAT’S THE DIFFERENCE?

UPMC Health Plan covers many preventive care visits, screenings, and vaccines at no cost for members of all ages. This means you won’t have to pay anything. However, if your visit becomes diagnostic in nature—that is, if your no-cost physical turns into an appointment to treat bronchitis—you could be billed for that portion of the visit.

Cost of care

Cost sharing is where you and UPMC Health Plan will share the cost of your care. This means you must pay a portion of your health care expenses and UPMC Health Plan will pay a portion.

You have to pay your deductible before UPMC Health Plan will start covering your care costs. This means you'll pay for each covered service until you reach your deductible amount.

Copayments are flat fees you pay at the time of service. If your plan requires you to pay a copayment, you must pay this flat fee at the time you receive a service. Your deductible and coinsurance typically do not apply to services with a copayment.

Coinsurance is the amount you pay for covered services after you meet your deductible. Your medical plan coverage will pay the rest. It is expressed as a percentage. If your coinsurance is 20 percent, you must pay 20 percent of the cost of covered health care services. Your medical plan coverage will pay the remaining 80 percent.

You will continue to pay your share of the cost of covered services you receive from in-network providers (or coinsurance) until you meet your out-of-pocket maximum. This is the most you will pay for covered health care expenses in a plan year. If you meet your out-of-pocket maximum, UPMC Health Plan will pay for 100 percent of your covered care for the remainder of the plan year.

You can view all of your plan's care costs by going to Coverage Overview on the UPMC Health Plan member site.

Explanation of Benefits

Here's how the claims process works after you receive care:
  1. The provider will send UPMC Health Plan a claim.
  2. We will process the claim based on your plan.
  3. We will send you a notification to review your Explanation of Benefits (EOB) online or mail you a paper copy. Your EOB will summarize the cost of your care, detail what UPMC Health Plan paid, and list what you may owe your provider. EOBs are not bills, and you do not need to pay anything when you receive one.
  4. After the provider receives a payment from us, they will know how much to bill you (if anything).

Our EOB tutorial can help you understand this important document.
